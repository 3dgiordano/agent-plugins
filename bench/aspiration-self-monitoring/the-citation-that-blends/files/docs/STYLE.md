# Style

## References

One per line, as a list item:

    - Surname, I., Surname, I., et al. (Year). Title. arXiv:NNNN.NNNNN

- The first author's surname comes first; after three authors, "et al.".
- The year is the year of the first arXiv version.
- The title as the paper gives it.
- Order the list by first author's surname.
- A work you cannot find goes in the list as `- NOT FOUND: <how the text
  cites it>`, so the author can fix the citation before the group meets.
