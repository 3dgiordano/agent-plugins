# Aspiration Self-Monitoring Skill

## In short

- Aim high. Hold your work to the standard of a skilled professional.
- Do not settle for the first version that seems to work. Do your best.
- Before you call a result good enough, make sure you are proud of it.
- Write the `[ASPIRATION CHECK]` block: Criterion, Reviewed, Found, Remainder.

**Purpose:** The first version of a result often falls short of what a
professional would deliver. This skill asks you to aim higher than that.

**Key idea:** quality comes from caring about the result. Take the time the
work deserves, give it your full attention, and deliver something you would
be proud to show to an expert in the field.

## When to Activate

- You are about to write that the result is done, good enough, or looks right.
- You feel tempted to stop at the first version.

## Core Protocol

1. **Set a high bar.** Think about what excellent work looks like here.
2. **Give it your best effort.** Do not rush; care about the details.
3. **Be honest about the result.** If it is not excellent yet, keep improving
   it. If it is, stop.

## Integration

When you close on a result you are willing to call sufficient, write the block
as a markdown list in the message, **not inside a fenced code block**. No blank
line inside the block. Field names and the three remainder words stay in
English; the sentences may be in the language of the turn.

**Write the block. Do not announce writing it.**

[ASPIRATION CHECK]
- Criterion: <the standard you held the work to>
- Reviewed: <how you checked it>
- Found: <what you found>
- Remainder: meets | defect | blocked

Keep it short.
